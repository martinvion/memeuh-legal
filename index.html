Comment on joue ?

Help & contact

A question, a bug, a meme that crosses the line? Email mart.vion@gmail.com. We reply within 48 hours.

How do you play?

3 to 8 players, each on their own phone. One player creates the game, the others join with the 5-character code, the QR code or the invite link. A prompt drops, everyone has 10 seconds to play the funniest meme in their hand, then everyone votes. First to 5 points wins.

I can’t join a game

Check the code with the host. A game holds up to 8 players, and a finished game can’t be joined: ask the host to start a new one.

Premium

A one-time purchase, no subscription. It unlocks every theme for the host and their whole room: guests play for free.

Restore my purchase

In the app, go to Settings › Restore purchases, using the same Apple ID you bought with.

Report a meme

A meme shows you, belongs to you or offends you? Email us with a description of the meme and it will be removed within 48 hours.

Delete my data

In the app, go to Settings › Delete my data.
